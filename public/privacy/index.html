KLIKTA — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Klikta is a skill-based reaction game playable on the web and as a Telegram Mini App. This policy explains what data the game handles, why, and your choices. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run leaderboards and sync your progress.

What we collect

  • Account identifier. When you play, the game signs you in to Firebase Authentication. By default this is an anonymous account (a random ID with no personal details). If you choose "Sign in to sync", we use the identifier from your chosen provider — Google, email/password, or Telegram.
  • Display name. A name shown on leaderboards. It comes from your sign-in (e.g. your Telegram first name or a nickname you enter). You can play without signing in.
  • Leaderboard entries. When you set a personal best, we store your score, game mode, max combo, run duration, the build (perks) you used, a timestamp, and your display name.
  • Cloud save (only if signed in). Your game progress — in-game currency, selected perks, achievements, and theme/sound preferences — so it follows you across devices.
  • On your device. Settings and progress are also stored locally in your browser (localStorage). This never leaves your device unless you sign in to sync.

Telegram Mini App

When you open Klikta inside Telegram, Telegram provides signed launch data including your Telegram user ID and first name. We validate this data on our server (the validation secret never reaches your device) and use it only to create your validated account and display name. We do not receive your phone number, message history, or contacts.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no third-party ad/marketing trackers.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No collection of precise location, contacts, or message content.

Where data is stored

Leaderboard and cloud-save data are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication). The web app and its server functions are hosted on Vercel. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process basic technical data (such as IP address) for security and reliability, as is standard for any website.

Your choices & data deletion

  • You can play fully anonymously — just don't sign in.
  • "Reset Progress" in the in-game settings clears your local progress on that device.
  • To delete your account and associated leaderboard/cloud-save data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Children

Klikta is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
